# RQ1 geography

AEI data are conversations matched to O*NET tasks. Nothing here says anything about job risk, displacement or reassurance about the labour market.

## α split

RQ1's α = 0.05 is split (D22): H2 is tested on its own at α = 0.0125, and {H1, H3, H4} by Holm at family-wise α = 0.0375, so the RQ1 family-wise error is at most 0.05. This document tests nothing; the split is stated because every RQ1 results document states it.

## Scope and reading

- Descriptive only: no test, no p-value and no estimator is attached to anything here. The US-state H4 regression is robustness R28 (Plan 4); this section does not run it.
- Release r6, source `claude_ai`. `automation_pct` is the geography's equal-weighted mean over the published months of the share of classified conversations in the directive and feedback-loop patterns.
- AUI is the Anthropic Usage Index: a geography's share of Claude usage divided by its share of working-age population.
- `mix` is the geography's task-mix control M_c (the geography's O*NET general work activity shares weighted by the global automation share of each activity).
- `in_h4_sample` is true iff the geography is in the confirmatory H4 frame (it has a GDP per working-age person). A geography without one stays in these tables and maps.
- A geography the release does not publish (a missing month, or no task mix) is not in the table; on the maps it is drawn in grey with the legend entry "not published", never as zero.
