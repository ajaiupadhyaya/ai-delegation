# Tercile portfolios (exploratory, PAP §8.3 D19; Design B rows are secondary)

Generated by `uv run python -m aideleg.rq3 terciles`. Do not edit by hand.

Exploratory analysis (PAP §8.3, D19). Design B rows are secondary and Design A rows exploratory; A_pooled is labelled look-ahead, because r3-r6 were published during and after the return window. Nothing here is a confirmatory test; the confirmatory H5/H5b decision in `docs/results/confirmatory_h5.md` stands as recorded.

These portfolios are measurement devices, not strategies (spec §5.4).

## Method

- Membership: FF49 industries with a non-null `z_delegation` (conversations matched to O*NET tasks, aggregated to FF49), k = n // 3; `high` is the top k and `low` the bottom k by exposure, ties broken by ascending `ff49` (PAP §11 C-34). Membership is fixed at formation.
- Legs: FF49 monthly value-weighted industry returns, in percent. Cap legs weight industry j in month t by its market capitalisation (average firm size x number of firms) at the prior month-end (PAP §8.3 P8, §11 C-35); EW legs weight equally (P19). An industry with a missing return, or under cap weighting a missing cap, leaves that month's leg and the weights renormalise over the rest. H-L = high minus low; it is a zero-investment return, so no rf is subtracted.
- Windows: Design B, 2025-04 to 2026-08 month ends; Design A, 2022-12 to 2026-08 month ends (inclusive).
- Alphas: OLS of monthly H-L on [1, factors], Newey-West SEs with lag floor(4 (T/100)^(2/9)); 95% CI and p from the t distribution with T - k - 1 df; x 12, in annualised pp. CAPM and FF5+UMD use the FF5 (2x3) and momentum files; FF3 uses the canonical FF3 file, never FF5 columns (PAP §11 C-36).
- Mean: annualised pp (x 12) with NW SE and t-based p; Sharpe: annualised (x sqrt 12); PSR(0) on the monthly Sharpe ratio. The 95% CIs of the mean and the Sharpe are stationary-bootstrap percentile intervals (10,000 draws, mean block 3 months), one draw set per variant from one `default_rng(SEED)` used in variant order (PAP §11 C-37).
- Costs: 0, 5, 15, 30 bps one-way on formation turnover (200% = 100% long plus 100% short), deducted once from the first month.
- DSR: each variant's PSR against the expected maximum monthly Sharpe ratio across the variants run (gross of costs).
- Every estimate is reported with its point value, CI and nominal p where one exists. No result is counted or starred; an interval that includes zero is reported in the same way as one that does not.
