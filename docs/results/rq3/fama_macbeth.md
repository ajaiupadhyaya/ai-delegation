## Method

- Dependent variable: the FF49 monthly value-weighted industry return minus rf (FF5 monthly file, matched by date), in percent.
- Exposure z: the design's `z_delegation` (conversations matched to O*NET tasks, aggregated to FF49), as given; it is not re-standardised per month or after drops.
- log size: ln(average firm size) at the prior month-end, from the frozen `ff49_monthly` file (PAP §11 C-35).
- log BE/ME: ln("Sum of BE / Sum of ME") from the frozen `ff49_monthly` file, French's July-June timing: the row for year y (BE for fiscal y - 1 over ME at December y - 1) is used from July y to June y + 1 (PAP §11 C-38).
- Momentum: the 12-2 cumulative return, prod over months t-12..t-2 of (1 + r/100) - 1, as a decimal, from the same value-weighted industry returns; missing unless all 11 returns are present.
- A non-positive or missing size or BE/ME, a missing return or a missing exposure removes that industry from that month's regression (listed below).
- Each month: OLS of the excess return on [1, z, log size, log BE/ME, momentum] across the complete industries. A month with n <= 5 complete industries is skipped and counted (PAP §11 C-38).
- Each coefficient: the time-series mean of the monthly slopes, Newey-West SE with lag floor(4 (T/100)^(2/9)) (Bartlett kernel, no small-sample correction), p and 95% CI from the t distribution with T - 1 df.
- Units: the slope on z is pp per month per 1 SD of exposure, with x 12 (annualised pp per 1 SD) beside it; the other slopes are pp per month per unit of the regressor (per unit of log size, of log BE/ME, and of the cumulative return as a decimal).
- Windows: Design B, 2025-04 to 2026-08 month ends; Design A, 2022-12 to 2026-08 month ends (inclusive). A_r2 is formed from r2, the earliest release with task-level collaboration. A_pooled pools r2-r6; r3-r6 were published during and after the return window, so it is labelled look-ahead.
- Every estimate is reported with its point value, CI and nominal p. No result is counted or starred; an interval that includes zero is reported in the same way as one that does not.

## Caveats

- Design B and Design A_r2 exposures are numerically identical (both are formed from release r2; PAP §8.3 D19), so the B regressions are the A_r2 regressions, with the same industries and exposure values, over a 17-month sub-window (2025-04 to 2026-08) of A_r2's 45 months. They are not independent evidence.
