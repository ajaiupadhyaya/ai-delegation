## Method

- T_j = talk of FF49 industry j in 10-Ks filed 2024-01-01 → 2024-12-31, all public before Design B's formation date, 2025-03-27 (PAP §6 D18; see `talk_index.md`).
- Industries: the FF49 industries with T (49). Industries with no 2024 filer (T undefined): none. Unmapped filers enter no industry: 978 of 6774 2024 filers (14.44%).
- Unmapped filers with a SIC outside Siccodes49 are mostly SIC 6189 (asset-backed securities issuers): 5,461 of 5,580 filer-years over 2019–2025 (97.87%; 97.26% to 98.62% by year). The unmapped share of filers moves from 11.92% (2019) to 15.81% (2025), so part of the change in the all-filers pooled talk share is a change in the filers' composition; the mapped-filers column of `talk_index.md` leaves the unmapped filers out and isolates the trend among filers assigned to an industry.
- z_usage: Design B (release r2) `z_usage`, conversations matched to O*NET tasks aggregated to FF49 (PAP §2.1), from the hash-checked `rq3.data.exposures()`; used as given, not re-standardised over the industries with T.
- zT = (T − mean) / population SD across those industries; OLS of zT on [1, z_usage]; gap_j = the residual divided by its population SD. gap_j > 0: the industry's talk is higher than its z_usage predicts in this cross-section.
- Divergence: Spearman(T, z_usage), average ranks for ties, with a 95% percentile CI from 10,000 industry-bootstrap draws (industries resampled with replacement as (T, z_usage) pairs, `default_rng(SEED)`); draws with a constant T or z_usage are undefined, dropped and counted.
- Zero-assigned employment share, Design B: 12.14% (D3)
