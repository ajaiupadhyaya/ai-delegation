# H6 secondary talk measure per 10,000 words of the 10-K primary document — exploratory (PAP §6, D18)

Generated by `uv run python -m aideleg.h6 secondary`. Do not edit by hand.

Dictionary occurrences (primary terms and gated acronyms, PAP §6.1 D12) per 10,000 words of the primary 10-K document downloaded from EDGAR, firm-level, then the industry mean (PAP §6 D18, C-41). It measures what filings say, not what firms do. Descriptive only: no interval and no test is attached to it here.

## Definitions

- Filings: every D18 filing 2019–2025 (`filers.parquet`, C-41, C-49), one primary document per accession number; a co-registrant filing gives the same document to each of its CIKs.
- Text: HTML documents (inline XBRL included) through `clean_text` (C-48); plain-text documents through `clean_text(html=False)`. Matching: `count_terms` (D12, C-48).
- Filing rate = 10,000 × (primary hits + gated acronym hits) / words. A filing with zero words, no primary document, a PDF or another type, or no saved document has no rate and is left out (counted below); filings under 2,000 words are kept.
- Firm-year rate: the mean over the CIK's filings with a rate in the year. Industry rate: the equal-weight mean over the CIKs of the FF49 industry with a firm-year rate. Unmapped CIKs (no SIC, or a SIC outside Siccodes49) enter no industry.
- Defaults the PAP leaves open are fixed in PAP §11 (the Task 13 clarification).
