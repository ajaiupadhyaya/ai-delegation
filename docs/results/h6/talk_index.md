# H6 talk index by filing year — exploratory (PAP §6, D18)

Generated by `uv run python -m aideleg.h6 talk`. Do not edit by hand.

Talk for FF49 industry j and filing year y is the share of the year's 10-K filers in j whose 10-K is returned by EDGAR full-text search for at least one primary term (PAP §6 D18, §6.1 D12). It measures what filings say, not what firms do.

## Definitions

- Numerator: distinct CIKs among the year's filers with at least one full-text-search hit in a 10-K, 10-K405 or 10-KT (amendments excluded) filed in the year, any document of the filing (C-39, C-50). Exhibit-only talk filers are counted in it and also shown apart (C-39).
- Denominator: distinct CIKs filing one of those form types in the year, from the EDGAR full index (C-49).
- Industry: the CIK's current SIC (C-40) mapped to FF49 by the Siccodes49 ranges. A CIK with no SIC, or a SIC outside every range, is unmapped: it is in the denominator's `ff49 = null` row, never in an industry (C-49).
- Pooled talk: total talk filers over total filers of the year, unmapped filers included; the mapped-only share is shown beside it.
- Hit CIKs that are not among the year's full-index filers are listed by `edgar.out_of_denominator` and never counted (C-50).
