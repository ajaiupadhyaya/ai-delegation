## Notes

- [default] The unbalanced panel keeps every (task, release) row with a non-null outcome for tasks with such rows in at least two of the listed releases (Plan 2's `build_h2_panel(balanced=False)`); there is no other unbalanced rule (PAP §11 C-56).
- H2 parts: Plan 2's `estimate_h2` on Plan 2's `build_h2_panel` (through the adapter), with H2's inference (PAP §4.2, D22, §8.1): WLS of the outcome on t_r and task dummies with fixed task weights (the task's mean `pct` over the listed releases), SEs clustered by task, t with G_task − 1 df; `p` is the two-sided task-clustered p of the trend. The note gives the restricted wild cluster bootstrap p (clusters = releases, Webb weights, every one of the 6^k weight vectors enumerated; PAP D22, §11 C-15) and whether the D22 rule's conditions would hold (both p ≤ 0.0125, compared exactly as PAP §11 C-24, and β > 0): a mechanical statement, not a decision; the release-label randomisation p is descriptive. The smallest attainable wild-bootstrap p is 6/6^k: 3 releases, 6/216 = 0.0278, above 0.0125, so the wild condition cannot hold; 4 releases, 6/1296 = 0.0046; 5 releases, 6/7776 = 0.0008. β is the change in measured within-task outcome per year, including any change in the classifier and platform scope (PAP §5). Coefficients are in pp of the outcome share per year. Each table row's note cell starts with its estimand (PAP §11 C-78). `n` is the number of (task, release) rows; `dropped` shows none and `n_dropped` is not reported for H2 rows, because Plan 2's H2 builder applies the sample rule and records no drops (PAP §11 C-78).
