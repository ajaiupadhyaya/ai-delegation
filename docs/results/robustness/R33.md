# R33: robustness row (PAP §8.2 R33)

Source: PAP §8.2, row R33. Label: robustness.

Every record below is reported as robustness; not a confirmatory decision (PAP §8.1).

Units: correlation; pp of annualised alpha per 1 SD. Estimates and scales are formatted to 2 decimals (correlations to 3), p to 3 decimals; the CSV keeps full precision.

## Notes

- Variant: ExposureSpec(crosswalk='emp_weighted'); every other construction choice is the primary Design B one.
- Employment-weighted NAICS→SIC crosswalk from OEWS `nat5d_6d` (PAP D23, §8.2 R33). Children without published employment share the residual max(E₄ − Σ published, 0) equally; with no published child, the primary equal split (PAP §11 C-67).
- Benchmarks: AIOE and Eloundou are re-aggregated with the variant's matrix, crosswalk and industry set, so both sides of H5b use the same weights (PAP §11 C-65).
- Zero-assigned employment share (D3): 12.14%
- Market part: H5 and H5b on ff49_vw with s, b̄ and q recomputed for this exposure vector on the same 378 pre-seal windows, the t₂₁ rule and the D21 null b₀ = b̄ (PAP §3.3, §8.1); z is the variant's own and is not re-standardised after a drop.
- RQ2 part: Spearman correlations of the variant AEI indices with the variant benchmarks; the intervals are 2.5/97.5 percentile industry-bootstrap intervals on Plan 2's Design B draw set `bootstrap_indices(n)["B"]` (PAP §11 C-68). RQ2 is descriptive.
