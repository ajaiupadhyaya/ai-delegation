Source: PAP §8.2, row R10. Label: robustness.

Every record below is reported as robustness; not a confirmatory decision (PAP §8.1).

Units: pp of annualised alpha per 1 SD; pp of the outcome share per unit of the regressor. Estimates and scales are formatted to 2 decimals (correlations to 3), p to 3 decimals; the CSV keeps full precision.

## Notes

- Market parts of R10: the D1 suppression bounds `z_delegation_lb` and `z_delegation_ub` (built from `automation_pct_lb` / `_ub`) in place of `z_delegation`, for H5 and H5b (PAP §8.2 R10, D1). The H1 and H3 parts of R10 are RQ1 rows (PAP §11 C-57).
- Exposure: the primary Design B vector (`ExposureSpec()`). H5 and H5b with s, b̄ and q recomputed for this row by the pre-seal procedure (the same 378 windows unless the row says otherwise, the t₂₁ rule and the D21 null b₀ = b̄; PAP §3.3, §8.1); industries with a missing return in the confirmatory window are dropped and z is not re-standardised. The secondary b = 0 statistic is in each record's note.
- Zero-assigned employment share (D3): 12.14% (the primary Design B exposure; this row rebuilds no index).
- RQ1 parts of R10 (PAP §8.2 R10, D1): H1 with `automation_share_all_lb` / `_ub` in place of `automation_pct` (parts h1_lb, h1_ub); Plan 2's builder computes the bounds per period with r6's suppression component (`none_pct`) and then D13-averages them (PAP §11 C-4).
- [default] H3 with `validation_share_all_lb` = v·(100 − s)/100 and `_ub` = lb + s in place of `validation_pct` (parts h3_lb, h3_ub), v = `validation_pct` and s = r6's suppression share (`none_pct`), per period and then D13-averaged, as for the automation bounds (PAP §11 C-57).
- RQ1 parts: Plan 2's `estimate_h1` / `estimate_h3` on Plan 2's `build_task_rows` (through the adapter), with the inference of the hypothesis varied (PAP §8.1): WLS with IWA fixed effects unless the row says otherwise, two-way cluster-robust SEs by occupation (soc6) and task, t with min(G_occ, G_task) − 1 df and the PAP §4 non-PSD rule (each use is in the record's note). `scale` is the SE, `stat` the t statistic, intervals 95%, p two-sided; p_H1 = max(p_educ, p_wage) (D17). Coefficients are in pp of the outcome share per unit of the regressor. `n` is the number of (task, occupation, family) rows; `dropped` lists the non-zero drop shares in % of total task usage, structural then variable-level (PAP §4.1, §11 C-2, C-30).
