# R29: robustness row (PAP §8.2 R29)

Source: PAP §8.2, row R29. Label: robustness; exploratory.

Every record below is reported as robustness; not a confirmatory decision (PAP §8.1).

Units: correlation; pp of annualised alpha per 1 SD of gap. Estimates and scales are formatted to 2 decimals (correlations to 3), p to 3 decimals; the CSV keeps full precision.

## Notes

- [default] pre_formation_2025: the numerator hits (file date) and the denominator filers (Date Filed) are both cut at 2025-03-26, the filings public before Design B's formation date 2025-03-27 (PAP §11 C-61). pre_formation_2025: 2,456 talk filers of 4,262 filers (3,546 mapped to an FF49 industry); T_j undefined: none.
- change_2019_2024: T_j = talk₂₀₂₄ − talk₂₀₁₉, each from the full calendar year's 10-Ks (a difference of two shares of filers); T_j undefined: none. 2024: 2,538 talk filers of 6,774 filers (5,796 mapped to an FF49 industry); T_j undefined: none. 2019: 558 talk filers of 6,793 filers (5,983 mapped to an FF49 industry); T_j undefined: none.
- [default] float_weighted: T_j = Σ float·1[talks] / Σ float over industry j's 2024 filers with a public float in the SEC XBRL frame dei/EntityPublicFloat/USD/CY2024Q2I (the R29 cap proxy, a §11 addition outside §9.4, A-46; PAP §11 C-61); T_j is undefined where that sum is not positive. Filers with a public float: 4,159 of 6,774 2024 filers (61.40%); 4,062 of 5,796 FF49-mapped filers (70.08%); 244 of them report a float of zero and enter with weight zero. T_j undefined: none.
- Each part replaces Plan 3's T_j (talk in 10-Ks filed in 2024, the 15 primary phrases, PAP §6 D18) by the part's T_j; everything after T_j is Plan 3's code (PAP §11 C-51): gap_j = the standardised residual of zT on Design B (r2) z_usage (`h6.talk.gap`); divergence = Spearman(T_j, z_usage) with a 95% percentile CI from 10,000 industry-bootstrap draws (`default_rng(SEED)`); the gap–return association = `h6.talk.gap_returns`: OLS of the Design B window alphas on gap_j with s, b̄ and q recomputed for the gap vector on the same pre-seal historical windows, critical max(t₂₁, q), primary null b = b̄ (D21), b = 0 secondary (in the note); the interval is b ± critical × s.
- H6 is exploratory (PAP §6 D18): the p-values are nominal, belong to no testing family and do not revisit the H5/H5b decision. An FF49 industry with T_j undefined is excluded from zT, the OLS, Spearman and the gap–return regression (C-51 (2)); unmapped filers enter no industry (C-49 (5)).
- Hits come from the saved EDGAR full-text-search responses (Plan 3's `hits_from_saved`, strict: a date range the saved leaf ranges do not cover is an error, never zero hits).
- Zero-assigned employment share, Design B: 12.14% (D3)
