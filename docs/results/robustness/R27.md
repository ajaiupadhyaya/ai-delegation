# R27: robustness row (PAP §8.2 R27)

Source: PAP §8.2, row R27. Label: robustness.

Every record below is reported as robustness; not a confirmatory decision (PAP §8.1).

Units: pp of the outcome share per unit of the regressor. Estimates and scales are formatted to 2 decimals (correlations to 3), p to 3 decimals; the CSV keeps full precision.

## Notes

- [default] H3 with the O*NET 29.3 Work Context element Consequence of Error (`CX`, the mean `Data Value` over the O*NET-SOC codes within soc6) in place of the D15 indicator, z-scored across the distinct soc6 codes of the H3 rows that have a value (population SD), so γ is in pp of validation share per 1 SD (PAP §8.2 R27, §11 C-58). Rows without a value are dropped and reported as `missing_coe`.
- 22 of the 391 soc6 codes of the H3 rows have no Consequence of Error value.
- RQ1 parts: Plan 2's `estimate_h1` / `estimate_h3` on Plan 2's `build_task_rows` (through the adapter), with the inference of the hypothesis varied (PAP §8.1): WLS with IWA fixed effects unless the row says otherwise, two-way cluster-robust SEs by occupation (soc6) and task, t with min(G_occ, G_task) − 1 df and the PAP §4 non-PSD rule (each use is in the record's note). `scale` is the SE, `stat` the t statistic, intervals 95%, p two-sided; p_H1 = max(p_educ, p_wage) (D17). Coefficients are in pp of validation share per 1 SD of Consequence of Error. Each table row's note cell starts with its coefficient (PAP §11 C-78). `n` is the number of (task, occupation, family) rows; `dropped` lists the non-zero drop shares in % of total task usage, structural then variable-level (PAP §4.1, §11 C-2, C-30); `n_dropped` is not reported for RQ1 rows, because Plan 2 records drops as usage shares, not row counts (PAP §11 C-2, C-78).
