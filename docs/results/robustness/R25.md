# R25: robustness row (PAP §8.2 R25)

Source: PAP §8.2, row R25. Label: robustness.

Every record below is reported as robustness; not a confirmatory decision (PAP §8.1).

Units: pp of the outcome share per unit of the regressor. Estimates and scales are formatted to 2 decimals (correlations to 3), p to 3 decimals; the CSV keeps full precision.

## Notes

- R25 (PAP §4.0 D17 alternative, §8.2 R25): H1 and H4 split into separate coefficients, giving the five-member Holm family {H1a: education, H1b: log wage, H3: error cost, H4a: log AUI, H4b: log income}, tested by Plan 2's `holm` at family-wise 0.0375 (D22): the k-th smallest p of the five (ties by name) is compared with 0.0375/(5 − k + 1), step-down, by exact comparison on each float's decimal repr. Each p is the committed P-RQ1 fit's two-sided coefficient p (`confirmatory_rq1.json`): H1's two-way cluster t p-values, H3's indicator p, and H4's normal-reference HC3 p-values of log AUI and log income. Nothing is re-estimated; the estimates, SEs and intervals are the committed ones.
- H2 is outside this family and is tested on its own at 0.0125 (D22). 'rejected under R25' is the Holm step alone, with no sign condition; it is reported only and is not a decision: the RQ1 decisions are P-RQ1's (D17).
- Coefficients are in pp of the outcome share per unit of the regressor (H3: validation share). Each table row's note cell starts with its coefficient (PAP §11 C-78). `n` is the committed primary sample: (task, occupation, family) rows for H1 and H3, countries for H4; `dropped` shows none and `n_dropped` is not reported, because the samples are the primary ones (PAP §11 C-78).
