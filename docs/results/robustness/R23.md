# R23: robustness row (PAP §8.2 R23)

Source: PAP §8.2, row R23. Label: part h1 "robustness"; part h2 "robustness; confounded with the r6 suppression-regime change".

Every record below is reported as robustness; not a confirmatory decision (PAP §8.1).

Units: ai_autonomy_mean scale points per unit of the regressor; ai_autonomy_mean scale points per year. Estimates and scales are formatted to 2 decimals (correlations to 3), p to 3 decimals; the CSV keeps full precision.

## Notes

- `ai_autonomy_mean` (D13-averaged, r6 `claude_ai`) in place of `automation_pct` as H1's outcome (PAP §8.2 R23, spec §5.4.1); same predictors, sample rule, fixed effects and inference. R23's H2 part is a separate Plan 4 task.
- R23 is reported beside H1 (PAP §4.1): the H1 decision uses `automation_pct` only and is unchanged. The PAP §4.1 disagreement sentence is printed below only if each R23 coefficient (education, log wage) has the sign opposite to the committed primary H1 coefficient (P-RQ1 record) and p < 0.05, a mechanical check (PAP §11 C-77).
- Sign check against the committed primary H1 (P-RQ1): education R23 0.03 vs primary -0.53, opposite sign, p < 0.001; log wage R23 0.03 vs primary 4.40, same sign, p 0.177 (PAP §11 C-77)
- RQ1 parts: Plan 2's `estimate_h1` / `estimate_h3` on Plan 2's `build_task_rows` (through the adapter), with the inference of the hypothesis varied (PAP §8.1): WLS with IWA fixed effects unless the row says otherwise, two-way cluster-robust SEs by occupation (soc6) and task, t with min(G_occ, G_task) − 1 df and the PAP §4 non-PSD rule (each use is in the record's note). `scale` is the SE, `stat` the t statistic, intervals 95%, p two-sided; p_H1 = max(p_educ, p_wage) (D17). Coefficients are in ai_autonomy_mean scale points per unit of the regressor (per year of education, per unit of log wage). Each table row's note cell starts with its coefficient (PAP §11 C-78). `n` is the number of (task, occupation, family) rows; `dropped` lists the non-zero drop shares in % of total task usage, structural then variable-level (PAP §4.1, §11 C-2, C-30); `n_dropped` is not reported for RQ1 rows, because Plan 2 records drops as usage shares, not row counts (PAP §11 C-2, C-78).
- H2 part (part h2; Plan 4 Task 12): `ai_autonomy_mean` as H2's outcome over r4–r6, the releases over which it is definition-stable (PAP §5 D2, §8.2 R23), balanced over the three; the series includes r6 against D2's regime caveat and has no r6 indicator, so it is labelled "confounded with the r6 suppression-regime change", like R22's all-releases series. The H1 part is Task 11's (commit 61b0625), carried verbatim and not re-estimated.
- H2 parts: Plan 2's `estimate_h2` on Plan 2's `build_h2_panel` (through the adapter), with H2's inference (PAP §4.2, D22, §8.1): WLS of the outcome on t_r and task dummies with fixed task weights (the task's mean `pct` over the listed releases), SEs clustered by task, t with G_task − 1 df; `p` is the two-sided task-clustered p of the trend. The note gives the restricted wild cluster bootstrap p (clusters = releases, Webb weights, every one of the 6^k weight vectors enumerated; PAP D22, §11 C-15) and whether the D22 rule's conditions would hold (both p ≤ 0.0125, compared exactly as PAP §11 C-24, and β > 0): a mechanical statement, not a decision; the release-label randomisation p is descriptive. The smallest attainable wild-bootstrap p is 6/6^k: 3 releases, 6/216 = 0.0278, above 0.0125, so the wild condition cannot hold. β is the change in measured within-task outcome per year, including any change in the classifier and platform scope (PAP §5). Coefficients are in ai_autonomy_mean scale points per year. Each table row's note cell starts with its estimand (PAP §11 C-78). `n` is the number of (task, release) rows; `dropped` shows none and `n_dropped` is not reported for H2 rows, because Plan 2's H2 builder applies the sample rule and records no drops (PAP §11 C-78).
