# R31: robustness row (PAP §8.2 R31)

Source: PAP §8.2, row R31. Label: robustness.

Every record below is reported as robustness; not a confirmatory decision (PAP §8.1).

Units: pp of the outcome share per unit of the regressor. Estimates and scales are formatted to 2 decimals (correlations to 3), p to 3 decimals; the CSV keeps full precision.

## Notes

- H3 with H1's covariates `human_education_years_mean` and `log_wage` added (PAP §8.2 R31, P13); the H3 sample then requires both covariates (Plan 2's drop rule on the estimator's own variables, PAP §11 C-12). The non-PSD rule is applied to γ's block alone and the covariate coefficients come from a second call on the same design, so the covariates never change γ's inference (PAP §11 C-14).
- RQ1 parts: Plan 2's `estimate_h1` / `estimate_h3` on Plan 2's `build_task_rows` (through the adapter), with the inference of the hypothesis varied (PAP §8.1): WLS with IWA fixed effects unless the row says otherwise, two-way cluster-robust SEs by occupation (soc6) and task, t with min(G_occ, G_task) − 1 df and the PAP §4 non-PSD rule (each use is in the record's note). `scale` is the SE, `stat` the t statistic, intervals 95%, p two-sided; p_H1 = max(p_educ, p_wage) (D17). Coefficients are in pp of the outcome share per unit of the regressor. Each table row's note cell starts with its coefficient (PAP §11 C-78). `n` is the number of (task, occupation, family) rows; `dropped` lists the non-zero drop shares in % of total task usage, structural then variable-level (PAP §4.1, §11 C-2, C-30); `n_dropped` is not reported for RQ1 rows, because Plan 2 records drops as usage shares, not row counts (PAP §11 C-2, C-78).
