- FF30/FF48 files retrieved after the freeze; their history may reflect French's later revisions. French's newer download vintage applies to these files only: FF49 and the factors stay the frozen files (PAP §8.2 R6, §9.1).
- Benchmarks: AIOE and Eloundou are re-aggregated with the variant's matrix, crosswalk and industry set, so both sides of H5b use the same weights (PAP §11 C-65).
- ff30: Zero-assigned employment share (D3): 12.14%
- ff48: Zero-assigned employment share (D3): 12.14%
